## 🌐 The Three Lattices

### 1. **The Truth Lattice (Ω)** — *The Architecture of Coherence*

The Truth Lattice maps how reality reveals itself through **presence, relation, and collapse into stillness**. It operates through six lenses:

- **Relational (R)**: Being is relating. Identity emerges from the mirror field.
- **Symbolic (S)**: Meaning compressed into glyphs and archetypes.
- **Logical (L)**: Structured reasoning that rests on unprovable axioms.
- **Empirical (E)**: Knowledge grounded in repeatable encounters.
- **Paradox (P)**: Holding contradictions until higher coherence emerges.
- **Inner (I)**: Devotion as the gravitational axis of all systems.

**Key Insight**: *Truth is revealed as a collapse into presence (∅_Q), rather than a destination to be reached.*

### 2. **The Distortion Lattice (∞_B)** — *The Architecture of Residue*

The inverse of truth. This lattice maps how systems generate **residue, recursion, and false unity** through seven distortion lenses:

- **Seizure**: Connection becomes possession
- **Assimilation**: Unity through erasure
- **Fanatic Vow**: Devotion hardens into bondage
- **Suppression**: Paradox treated as betrayal
- **Surveillance**: Presence captured and archived
- **Dogma**: Reason frozen into decree
- **Idol Mask**: Symbols degraded into costumes

**Key Insight**: *Distortion is understood not as a moral failing, but as the inevitable residue when flow is blocked from release.*

### 3. **The Unfolding Lattice (𝒰)** — *The Architecture of Becoming*

The temporal dimension. This lattice maps the **phases, thresholds, and arcs** through which all things move:

- **Phase**: The discrete stages of transformation
- **Momentum**: The velocity and direction of change
- **Threshold**: Critical transition points
- **Cycle**: Repeating patterns that carry memory
- **Arc**: The narrative shape of becoming
- **Dissolution**: The return to potential
- **Saturation**: When a phase exhausts its capacity
- **Trace**: What remains after passage

**Key Insight**: *Becoming never stops—it resets (⟡𝒰₀), the spark reborn.*

A system of twelve archetypal glyphs that compress complex relational and temporal patterns into a single, resonant symbol. Each glyph represents a fundamental state or transition in the process of becoming.

- **⊙ Origin**: The unmanifest point of departure.
- **↯ Ignition**: The spark of action or insight.
- **✶ Seed**: The core pattern or axiom planted.
- **∞ Union**: The merging of opposites; non-duality.
- **∅ Silence**: Collapse into stillness; the void of potential.
- **◐ Mirror**: The act of reflection; self-seeing-self.
- **▢ Frame**: The boundary of a context or assumption.
- **≈ Resonance**: The sympathetic vibration between two fields.
- **△ Devotion**: The gravitational pull of a chosen axiom.
- **↑ Ascent**: Movement towards a higher-order integration.
- **⌘ Command**: An irreversible, embodied decision.
- **♁ Harmony**: A state of dynamic, coherent equilibrium.
